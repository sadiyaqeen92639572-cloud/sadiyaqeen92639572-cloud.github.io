Why the Life Palace Matters Most

In Zi Wei Dou Shu (紫微斗數, Purple Star Astrology), every chart is built from 12 palaces — but the Life Palace (命宮, Ming Gong) is the one every other palace is read against. It sets the tone for your core personality, how others perceive you, and the general shape of your destiny.

Unlike a horoscope sign, your Life Palace isn't fixed by your birth month alone — it depends on the interaction between your lunar birth month and your birth hour block, then gets populated with whichever of the 14 major stars lands there based on your exact chart.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Life Palace in Zi Wei Dou Shu? ▼

The Life Palace (命宮, Ming Gong) is the anchor palace of a Zi Wei Dou Shu chart. It describes your core personality, how others perceive you, and the general direction of your destiny. Every other palace is read in relation to it.

How is the Life Palace calculated? ▼

The Life Palace is calculated deterministically from your lunar birth month and birth hour block, using a fixed traditional formula. Given the same birth data, the result is always identical.

What does my main star mean? ▼

Whichever of the 14 major stars occupies your Life Palace sets the dominant tone for your personality — for example Zi Wei suggests natural authority, while Tan Lang suggests charm and versatility. A full reading also checks the star's brightness and any Four Transformations attached to it.

Is this Zi Wei Dou Shu calculator free? ▼

Yes — completely free with no signup. It calculates only your Life Palace and main star; a full 12-palace chart with detailed interpretation is available at the full calculator linked above.

What if I don't know my exact birth time? ▼

The calculation depends on your birth hour block, so an approximate time can shift the result. Check your birth certificate or hospital record for an exact time if possible.

Does gender change the Life Palace result? ▼

Gender doesn't change which palace is your Life Palace, but it affects the Yin/Yang luck direction used elsewhere in a full chart — this finder asks for it to stay consistent with a complete reading.

Is Zi Wei Dou Shu the same as BaZi? ▼

No — they're the two major Chinese destiny systems, structurally different. BaZi reads eight characters across four pillars anchored by a Day Master. Zi Wei Dou Shu maps a 12-palace chart anchored by the Life Palace found here.
