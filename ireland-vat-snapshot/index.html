This page handles one case — a single amount at the standard rate. In practice VAT in Ireland has several rates and a registration threshold that matter for real transactions:

  • 13.5% reduced rate: fuel (coal, home heating oil), construction, repair and maintenance services.
  • 9% second reduced rate: restaurant/catering food and hairdressing (moved from 13.5% on 1 July 2026).
  • 4.8% livestock/flat rate: agricultural supplies — a separate farmer addition, not a retail rate.
  • VAT registration threshold: €85,000 for goods, €42,500 for services — whether your business needs to register at all.
  • Cross-border rules: exports, EU intra-community supplies, and Northern Ireland/Windsor Framework treatment aren't modelled by a quick snapshot.
