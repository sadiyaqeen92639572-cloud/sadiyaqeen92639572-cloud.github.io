Frequently Asked Questions

Can a non-resident open a bank account fully online?

It depends on the country and the bank. Some jurisdictions (Switzerland via Swissquote/Dukascopy, Cyprus, Belize, Singapore private banks) allow full remote onboarding for non-residents. Others (UAE, Georgia, Hong Kong traditional banks) still require a branch visit.

Which countries let non-residents open a bank account remotely?

Based on sourced research: Singapore (private banks), Switzerland (Swissquote, Dukascopy), Cyprus, and Belize allow remote or largely remote opening. UAE, Panama, Georgia, and Hong Kong traditional banks typically require an in-person visit.

What is the minimum deposit to open a non-resident bank account?

It varies widely by country and bank tier, from no minimum (Georgia, some Cyprus accounts) to hundreds of thousands for private banking. Select a country above to see the sourced range.

How long does it take a non-resident to open a bank account abroad?

From 48 hours (Hong Kong fintech accounts) to several weeks (private banks with enhanced due diligence), depending on the country and the bank's compliance process.
