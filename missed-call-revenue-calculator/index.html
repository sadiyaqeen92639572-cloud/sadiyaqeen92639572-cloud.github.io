Why a missed call is usually a lost customer, not a delayed one

When a first-time caller reaches voicemail, most of them hang up and dial the next business on the list. Widely cited phone-behavior surveys put the share of callers who refuse to leave a voicemail at around 80%, and callback rates on the messages that do get left are low. For an appointment-based or trade business, that makes every unanswered new-customer call a near-direct revenue loss — not a call you will simply catch later.

This calculator turns that into a monthly and annual figure using four inputs you can estimate in a minute, then compares it against the price of an AI receptionist that answers every call.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate the cost of missed calls?

Multiply missed calls per week by 4.33 to get a monthly figure, multiply that by the share of new-customer calls that would have booked, then multiply by the average value of a new customer. That gives lost revenue per month. Multiply by 12 for the annual figure.

What percentage of callers leave a voicemail?

Industry surveys commonly report that roughly 80% of callers who reach voicemail hang up without leaving a message, and most of those never call back. That is why a missed call is usually a lost customer, not a delayed one.

How much does an AI receptionist cost?

Entry AI receptionist plans generally start around $20 to $60 per month for low call volume, with mid-tier plans in the $100 to $300 range once you add warm call transfers, calendar booking, and CRM sync. Usage-based platforms charge roughly $0.10 to $0.16 per minute instead of a flat fee. The cost breakdown on voiceagentcompare.com has the current per-vendor figures.

What answer-recovery rate does this calculator assume?

It assumes an AI receptionist answers and holds 90% of the calls you currently miss. The remaining 10% covers callers who hang up on any non-human greeting or misdial. You can treat the output as conservative if your AI setup routes urgent calls to a live person.

Is an AI receptionist worth it for a small business?

It depends on your call volume and average customer value. If you miss even a handful of new-customer calls a week and each customer is worth a few hundred dollars, the recovered revenue usually exceeds the monthly cost several times over. Low-volume businesses with cheap transactions see a smaller margin.

Does this calculator account for after-hours calls?

Indirectly. Enter every call you currently miss, including nights and weekends. An AI receptionist answers 24/7, so after-hours calls are part of what it recovers.

Is this an exact prediction of my revenue?

No. It is a deterministic estimate based on the numbers you enter. Real results depend on your close rate on answered calls, how well the AI is configured for your business, and seasonality. Use it as a starting point for a build-versus-buy decision, not a guarantee.
