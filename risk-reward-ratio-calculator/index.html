How This Calculator Works

This tool answers the question every trader should ask before entering a position: "is this trade worth taking?" It compares your potential loss (entry to stop) against your potential gain (entry to target), and shows the win rate you'd need just to break even at that ratio.

Calculation: deterministic arithmetic — no AI, no estimation

Formulas

risk = |entry − stop|
reward = |target − entry|
ratio = reward / risk
breakeven_win_rate = risk / (risk + reward)

Works for both long and short setups — the calculator uses absolute price distance, not direction.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate risk/reward ratio?

Divide your potential reward (target price minus entry) by your potential risk (entry minus stop price). A 1:3 ratio means you're risking $1 to make $3.

What is a good risk/reward ratio for trading?

Most traders target at least 1:2 (risk $1 to make $2), which keeps the required win rate under 50% to stay profitable.

How does risk/reward ratio relate to win rate?

Breakeven win rate = risk / (risk + reward). A 1:2 ratio needs only a 33% win rate to break even; a 1:1 ratio needs 50%.

Does a good R:R ratio guarantee profit?

No — R:R only tells you the breakeven win rate. Your actual win rate over enough trades determines real profitability.

Should I use risk/reward ratio for both long and short trades?

Yes — the same formula applies, just using stop-above-entry and target-below-entry for short positions.

Does this calculator tell me how many shares or contracts to trade?

No — this tool checks the ratio only. For exact position size in shares, lots, or futures contracts based on your account risk, use the full calculator linked below.
