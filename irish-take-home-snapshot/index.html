This page only covers the simplest case — one salary figure, standard credits, PRSI Class A at the pre-October rate. In practice more variables change the real number on your payslip:

  • Pay date after 1 October 2026: PRSI Class A rises from 4.20% to 4.35% — a calculator that lets you pick the pay date applies the correct rate automatically instead of a single fixed number.
  • Hourly or bonus pay: converting an hourly rate or a one-off bonus into an accurate net figure needs its own calculation path, not just the annual salary formula.
  • Self-employed / Schedule D income: follows PRSI Class S (4.20% of net profit, minimum €650/year) instead of Class A — a completely different formula from employee PAYE.
  • Redundancy payments: statutory redundancy is calculated and taxed separately from ordinary salary, with its own formula and exemptions.
  • Married or with extra credits: joint assessment, dependent credits, or medical credits all shift the PAYE band and the final net figure.

Irish Pay Calculator covers all of these: salary/take-home with hourly and bonus modes, statutory redundancy, and self-employed tax — each built on sourced 2026 Revenue.ie, gov.ie and WRC figures, with the PRSI rate change modelled automatically by pay date.
