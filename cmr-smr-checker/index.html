Why the WD Red Scandal Changed Everything

In April 2020, Blocks & Files revealed that Western Digital had silently switched WD Red 2TB–6TB drives from CMR to SMR — without changing the product name, packaging, or Amazon listing. NAS users buying replacement drives were unknowingly installing SMR drives into RAID arrays that previously ran CMR.

The consequences were severe: RAID rebuilds that previously took hours began taking days, then timing out. NAS units marked drives as failed mid-rebuild. Data loss followed. Western Digital eventually acknowledged the change and introduced the "WD Red Plus" branding for CMR variants — but the confusion persists because old WD Red stock still circulates on Amazon.

The lesson: never assume. Always verify CMR vs SMR before installing a drive in a NAS or RAID array. The checker above and the drive table are built for exactly this.
